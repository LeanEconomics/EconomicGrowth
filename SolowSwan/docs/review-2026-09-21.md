# Second review: Solow–Swan and Uzawa

The September 21, 2026 review compared the economic statements with the primary
sources, inspected the proof chains, rebuilt the libraries, and strengthened
the verification programs. No mathematical defect was found in the reviewed
principal results. This is a scoped review, not a claim that formalization
automatically establishes fidelity to an informal source.

## Mathematical and source review

- Solow–Swan: all 13 modules, covering the original Cobb–Douglas proofs and
  the general Acemoglu construction, positivity, uniqueness, stability,
  convergence, eight comparative statics, Golden Rule, stationary Cass
  comparison, saving changes, and effective-labor normalization.
- Uzawa: all six modules, covering the published Jones–Scrimgeour/Schlicht
  representation, the stronger domain-wide elasticity route, concrete
  examples, and the zero-investment obstruction for the earlier formulation.
- Source correspondence was checked against Acemoglu Chapter 2, the archived
  Solow article, Jones–Scrimgeour's 2004 and 2008 versions, and Schlicht (2006).

The existing scope restrictions remain essential: positive initial Solow
capital and positive dilution; additional saving restrictions for consumption;
stationary allocations rather than identical Cass transitions; and Uzawa's
explicit path/domain hypotheses. The original Uzawa (1961) article is discussed
but its full argument has not been formalized.

Corrected documentation includes Solow's depreciation reference (printed p. 66),
an inherited comment claiming an unexported continuous-dependence result,
verification working directories, and the location of Uzawa boundary examples.
Mathematical statements and proof bodies were unchanged by this review.

## Verification changes

The previous Uzawa verifier could omit declarations outside its limited scanner,
and Python optimization could disable its `assert` checks. All three growth
project verifiers now use explicit exceptions, require complete module and root
import inventories, scan the ordinary declaration forms used by these libraries,
and audit structure constructors and field projections as well as their types.
Unsupported declaration forms fail explicitly. Unused placeholders, new axioms,
unsafe declarations, and `native_decide` are rejected.

The previous Solow certificate check validated saved hashes and status records.
It now also checks diagnostic identities and classifications, freshly elaborates
all 20 certificate sources, checks their theorem types against the original
JSON propositions, and audits the fresh proof dependencies. An intentionally
substituted proof of `True`, with updated hashes and unchanged success metadata,
is rejected by actual Lean type checking in the regression suite.

The expanded audits cover **132 Solow theorems / 159 declarations** and
**66 Uzawa theorems / 107 declarations**. The larger declaration counts reflect
broader auditing, not additional economic results. Only `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` are accepted as axiom dependencies.
Source hashes and the latest successful run are recorded in each project's
`verification/verification.json`; GitHub Actions repeats the tests and audits.

TheoryDebugger's ten Solow diagnostics remain finite algebra checks. The ODE,
limit, production-function, and welfare reasoning is proved separately in Lean.
